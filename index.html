Common questions

Can I open an account without visiting a branch?

In this concept, you start online and finish at a branch where staff verify your ID in person.

What documents do I need?

Your original national ID and the reference number from your application. Students also bring a student ID.

Is there a minimum opening deposit?

This prototype does not set real fees or minimums. Final amounts would come from the bank.
